## Introduction

Welcome to Nexus Attendo! This system helps you mark your daily attendance quickly and easily using your mobile device or computer.

### What You Need

- A smartphone or computer with internet access
- GPS/Location services enabled
- Your registered email and password

## Getting Started

### First-Time Registration

1. **Open the App**
   - Navigate to your company's Nexus Attendo URL
   - Click on **Register**

2. **Fill Registration Form**
   - Enter your full name
   - Enter your company email address
   - Select your office location
   - Create a secure password
   - Confirm your password

3. **Submit Registration**
   - Click **Register**
   - You'll see a "Registration Pending" message
   - Wait for admin approval (usually within 24 hours)

4. **Approval Notification**
   - You'll receive an email when approved
   - You can now log in and mark attendance

### Logging In

1. Open the Nexus Attendo app
2. Enter your email address
3. Enter your password
4. Click **Login**
5. You'll be taken to your dashboard

### Forgot Password?

1. Click **Forgot Password** on login screen
2. Enter your registered email
3. Check your email for reset link
4. Click the link and create a new password
5. Log in with your new password

## Marking Attendance

### Daily Check-in Process

**Important:** You must check in during the attendance window (usually 9:30 AM - 6:00 PM) and be at the office location.

#### Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Open the App**
   - Log in to your account
   - You'll see the Dashboard

2. **Check Attendance Status**
   - Dashboard shows if you've already marked attendance
   - Shows current date and time
   - Displays attendance window timings

3. **Mark Attendance**
   - Click the **Mark Attendance** button
   - The app will request location permission

4. **Allow Location Access**
   - Click **Allow** when prompted
   - This is required to verify you're at the office
   - Your location is only used for attendance verification

5. **Wait for Verification**
   - The system verifies:
     - You're within the attendance window
     - You're at the office location (GPS check)
     - You haven't already marked attendance today

6. **Success!**
   - You'll see a success message
   - Your check-in time is recorded
   - Dashboard updates to show "Attendance Marked"

### Attendance Rules

**Timing:**
- **Attendance Window:** 9:30 AM - 6:00 PM (check with your admin)
- **Grace Period:** 15 minutes (default)
- **On-time:** Check in before 9:45 AM → Marked "Present"
- **Late:** Check in after 9:45 AM → Marked "Late"

**Location:**
- You must be at the office premises
- GPS must be enabled on your device
- You must be within the office geofence radius

**Check-out:**
- **Automatic:** System automatically checks you out at 6:00 PM
- **No manual check-out required**
- Your check-out time will show as 6:00 PM

### What If I Can't Check In?

**Outside Attendance Window:**
- You can only check in during the configured hours
- Contact your supervisor if you need to check in outside hours

**Outside Office Location:**
- You must be physically present at the office
- GPS must show you're within the office radius
- Contact admin if you believe this is an error

**Already Marked:**
- You can only check in once per day
- If you see "Already Marked," your attendance is recorded
- Check your history to verify

**Previous Day Not Checked Out:**
- If yesterday's check-out is missing, you cannot check in today
- Contact your admin to resolve this issue
- This ensures data accuracy

## Viewing Your Attendance

- **Check-out Time:** Automatic at 6:00 PM (shown after check-out)

**Navigation:** Bottom menu → History

View your past attendance records:
- **Date:** Each day's attendance
- **Status:** Present, Late, or Absent

- **Check-out Time:** When you left (6:00 PM automatic)

**Statistics:**
- Total Present days
- Total Late days
- Total Absent days

**Time Period:**
- Shows last 30 days by default
- Scroll to see older records

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### "Location Permission Denied"

**Solution:**
1. Go to your device settings
2. Find Nexus Attendo app
3. Enable Location/GPS permission
4. Refresh the app and try again

#### "You are not inside office premises"

**Possible Causes:**
- You're not at the office location
- GPS signal is weak
- Wrong office location assigned

**Solutions:**
1. Ensure you're physically at the office
2. Move near a window for better GPS signal
3. Wait a few seconds and try again
4. Contact admin if problem persists

#### "Attendance window is closed"

**Solution:**
- Check the attendance window timings on dashboard
- You can only check in during configured hours
- Contact supervisor if you need special permission

#### "Attendance already marked for today"

**Solution:**
- Your attendance is already recorded
- Check your dashboard or history to verify
- You can only check in once per day

#### "Previous day check-out is pending"

**Solution:**
- Yesterday's attendance wasn't properly checked out
- Contact your administrator to resolve
- This is a data integrity check

#### App Not Loading

**Solutions:**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Refresh the page (Ctrl + R or F5)
3. Clear browser cache
4. Try a different browser
5. Contact IT support

## FAQs

### General Questions

**Q: What time should I check in?**  
A: Check in during the attendance window (usually 9:30 AM - 6:00 PM). Arrive before the grace period ends to be marked "Present."

**Q: Do I need to check out?**  
A: No, the system automatically checks you out at 6:00 PM. No manual check-out is required.

**Q: Can I check in from home?**  
A: No, you must be physically present at the office. The system uses GPS to verify your location.

**Q: What if I forget to check in?**  
A: You'll be marked absent for that day. Contact your supervisor if there are extenuating circumstances.

**Q: Can I check in for someone else?**  
A: No, each employee must check in individually from their own device at the office location.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Why does the app need location access?**  
A: To verify you're at the office premises. Your location is only used for attendance verification and is not tracked otherwise.

**Q: Is my location data safe?**  
A: Yes, your location is only recorded at check-in time and stored securely. It's not continuously tracked.

**Q: What if GPS is not working?**  
A: Ensure location services are enabled on your device. Move to an area with better GPS signal (near windows). Contact admin if issues persist.

**Q: Can I use the app on multiple devices?**  
A: Yes, you can log in from any device, but you can only check in once per day.

**Q: What browsers are supported?**  
A: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge (latest versions). Mobile browsers are also supported.

### Attendance Questions

**Q: What's the difference between Present and Late?**  
A: Present = checked in within grace period. Late = checked in after grace period but within attendance window.

**Q: Will I be penalized for being late?**  
A: Attendance policies are set by your company. Check with your HR department.

**Q: Can I see my attendance rate?**  
A: Yes, view your history to see your Present, Late, and Absent statistics.

**Q: How far back can I see my attendance?**  
A: The history shows your last 30 days by default. Contact admin for older records.

**Q: What if I'm on leave?**  
A: Inform your supervisor and HR. Leave management is handled separately from daily attendance.
